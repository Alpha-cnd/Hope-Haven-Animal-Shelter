Team Bios

Sarah Mokoena | Founder & Director

Sarah established Hope Haven in 2015 after rescuing a severely injured stray dog on her way home from work. With a background in social work and a lifelong love of animals, she left her corporate job to build a sanctuary for Johannesburg's most vulnerable creatures. Under her leadership, Hope Haven has grown from a five kennel backyard operation to a registered NPC with two facilities and over 150 active volunteers. Sarah holds a certificate in Non Profit Management from Wits University and serves on the board of the South African Animal Welfare Association.

Rajesh Patel | Head Veterinarian

Dr. Patel joined Hope Haven in 2018, bringing 12 years of clinical experience from both private practice and wildlife rehabilitation centres. He oversees all medical care at the shelter, from routine vaccinations to complex surgical procedures. Dr. Patel is passionate about community education and runs monthly low cost spay/neuter clinics for pet owners in underserved areas. He holds a BVSc from the University of Pretoria and is registered with the South African Veterinary Council.

James Khumalo | Operations Manager

James manages the day to day running of the Main Shelter, coordinating staff schedules, supplies, and facility maintenance. A former logistics coordinator, James brings exceptional organisational skills to the team. He is also the primary liaison for corporate sponsors and donor relations. James believes that efficient operations mean more resources go directly to the animals. In his spare time, he fosters neonatal kittens a job that requires patience, warmth, and round the clock dedication.

Lindiwe Tshabalala | Volunteer Coordinator

Lindiwe joined Hope Haven as a volunteer in 2019 and quickly became indispensable. Today, she recruits, trains, and schedules over 150 volunteers across both facilities. She also manages the foster care program, matching vulnerable animals with temporary homes. Lindiwe has a diploma in Community Development and is currently studying part time toward a degree in Animal Behaviour. Her warm personality and organisational flair make her the heart of the volunteer community.

Program & Service Descriptions

Adoption Programme

Our adoption programme is the cornerstone of Hope Haven's mission. Every animal in our care receives a full veterinary assessment, vaccinations, sterilisation, and microchipping before being listed for adoption. Prospective adopters complete an enquiry form followed by a meet and greet session at the shelter. Our team provides post adoption support, including training referrals and veterinary advice, to ensure every placement is successful. Adoption fees help cover medical costs and fund the rescue of future animals.

Foster Care Programme

Not ready to adopt? Foster instead. Our foster programme places vulnerable animals particularly puppies, kittens, and recovering surgical patients into temporary homes where they receive one on one care. Hope Haven provides all food, medical supplies, and veterinary support. Foster periods range from two weeks to three months. This programme is critical to our capacity, as it frees up kennel space for new rescues while giving animals the socialisation they need to thrive.

Community Spay/Neuter Clinic

Held on the first Saturday of every month, our low cost clinic offers sterilisation, vaccination, and deworming services to pet owners in underserved communities. Prices are subsidised by donations and corporate sponsorships. Dr. Patel and a team of volunteer veterinary nurses typically treat 30-40 animals per clinic. Booking is essential and can be arranged via our Enquiry page or by calling the shelter directly.

Education & Outreach

Hope Haven visits local schools and community centres to teach responsible pet ownership, animal welfare, and bite prevention. Our interactive workshops are tailored for children aged 6-14 and include meet and greets with shelter animals. We believe that education is the most sustainable way to reduce abandonment and cruelty in our community.
