Max


Breed: Labrador Retriever Mix

Age: 3 years

Gender: Male

Size: Large

Personality

Friendly, Playful, Good with Children

Max arrived at Hope Haven in early 2025 after being found wandering near a construction site in Soweto. Despite his rough start, he has maintained an incredibly gentle and trusting nature. Max loves fetch, belly rubs, and will happily accompany you on morning jogs or afternoon naps. He is fully vaccinated, neutered, and house-trained. Max gets along wonderfully with children and other dogs, making him the perfect addition to an active family.

Ideal Home: A family with a garden and time for daily walks.

Adoption Fee: R850 (includes vaccination, sterilisation, and microchip)


Luna

Age: 2 years

Gender: Female

Size: Medium

Personality

Calm, Affectionate, Indoor Preference

Luna is litter-trained, spayed, and up to date on all vaccinations. She is not fond of loud environments but thrives in calm, loving homes. She will greet you with a soft purr and a slow blink the ultimate cat kiss.

Ideal Home: A quiet household, possibly with older children or adults only.

Adoption Fee: R650 (includes vaccination, sterilisation, and microchip)

Rocky

Breed: Staffordshire Bull Terrier

Age: 4 years

Gender: Male

Size: Medium

Personality

Energetic, Loyal, Intelligent

Rocky came to us through a cruelty investigation case. After months of rehabilitation and patient care from our veterinary team, he has blossomed into a confident, affectionate companion. Rocky is highly intelligent and responds well to training commands. He needs an experienced owner who can provide consistent boundaries and plenty of physical activity. Rocky is best suited as the only pet in the home but has endless love to give his human family.

Ideal Home: An experienced dog owner with an active lifestyle and secure yard.

Adoption Fee: R850 (includes vaccination, sterilisation, and microchip)

Thumper

Breed: Holland Lop Rabbit

Age: 1 year

Gender: Male

Size: Small

Personality

Gentle, Quiet, Child Friendly

Thumper was donated to Hope Haven by a local breeder who could no longer care for him. He is a charming, soft haired Holland Lop with a sweet temperament. Thumper enjoys fresh vegetables, supervised playtime outside his hutch, and being gently stroked. He is litter trained and accustomed to human handling. Rabbits make wonderful indoor pets for families who want something different from cats or dogs.

Ideal Home: An indoor home with space for a rabbit hutch and daily playtime.

Adoption Fee: R450 (includes health check and care guide)

Community Spay/Neuter Clinic

Held on the first Saturday of every month, our low-cost clinic offers sterilisation, vaccination, and deworming services to pet owners in underserved communities.

Prices are subsidised by donations and corporate sponsorships. Dr. Patel and a team of volunteer veterinary nurses typically treat 30-40 animals per clinic.

Booking is essential and can be arranged via our Enquiry page or by calling the shelter directly.
